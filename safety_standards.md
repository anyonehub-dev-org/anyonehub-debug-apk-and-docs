# Safety Standards & Compliance Policy

**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026  
**Organization:** Anyone-Hub LLC  
**Platform:** Anyone-Hub (Dev.io)

Anyone-Hub LLC is unconditionally committed to maintaining a safe, secure, and compliant digital environment for all users, developers, and collaborators. In compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies regarding child safety standards, safety reporting, and the prevention of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), we enforce strict operational standards across our platform.

---

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)
Anyone-Hub LLC maintains a strict **zero-tolerance policy** toward any content, behavior, or user activity associated with Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit:
* The creation, storage, transmission, or sharing of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
* Any form of grooming, exploitation, or endangerment of minors.
* The use of our infrastructure, APIs, tools, or real-time collaboration channels to distribute unlawful or harmful material targeting minors.

## 2. Platform Enforcement & Moderation
To uphold these standards, our platform incorporates rigorous technical safeguards:
* **Account Verification & Governance:** Access to collaborative workspaces requires authenticated account identifiers.
* **Proactive Monitoring & Reporting Channels:** Any detected violations or suspected instances of CSAE result in immediate account suspension, permanent revocation of platform access, and swift referral to lawological authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

## 3. Contact Information & Reporting
If you discover any content, behavior, or platform usage that violates our safety standards or involves suspected child exploitation, you can report it directly to our security and compliance team 24/7. 

* **Compliance & Support Email:** [anyone-hub.dev@zohomail.com](mailto:anyone-hub.dev@zohomail.com)
* **Response Time:** Urgent safety and CSAE reports are triaged and investigated within 24 hours.
